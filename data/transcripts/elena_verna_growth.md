### On the Fundamental Architecture of Growth Loops
**Elena Verna**:
"The biggest mental shift growth leaders must make is moving from linear funnels (Acquisition -> Activation -> Retention -> Referral -> Revenue) to compounding Growth Loops. 

Funnels lose energy at every single step. You pour 1,000 visitors in the top, 100 sign up, 10 activate, 1 pays. To get more revenue, you must constantly dump more money into top-of-funnel paid marketing. That creates an unsustainable treadmill.

A Growth Loop is a closed system where the output of one cohort of users becomes the input for the next cohort:
1. User joins and takes an action (e.g. creates a board on Miro or creates a survey on SurveyMonkey).
2. The action produces a natural artifact or shareable asset (inviting collaborators or distributing a survey link).
3. New prospects interact with the asset and experience the core value of the product.
4. A percentage of those prospects convert into new registered users, who then create their own assets.

When your loop reinvests output into new input, your customer acquisition cost (CAC) plummets and your growth compounds organically."

### On Freemium vs. Free Trial in B2B PLG
**Elena Verna**:
"Companies constantly ask: should we offer a 14-day free trial or a freemium tier?

Here is the heuristic:
- Use **Freemium** when you have a broad top-of-funnel, your product has low marginal cost to support free users, and value is realized quickly or through collaboration (like Miro, Figma, Slack). Freemium maximizes user volume and allows habitual usage to build over months before hitting a paywall.
- Use **Free Trial (Time-bound)** when your product is complex, has high support/infrastructure overhead, or when urgency is required to force an evaluation (like enterprise security or specialized developer infrastructure).
- The modern hybrid: **Reverse Trial**. You give the user full access to the Pro tier for 14 days. If they don't buy, they are downgraded to a permanent Free tier with feature limits rather than locked out. This captures immediate high-intent buyers while keeping long-tail users in your growth loop."

### On Product-Led Sales (PLS) and PQLs
**Elena Verna**:
"PLG does not mean 'no sales team'. PLG feeds Sales. 

In classic top-down sales, reps call cold leads from marketing forms (MQLs). In Product-Led Sales (PLS), reps reach out to Product Qualified Leads (PQLs)—people who have already signed up for the free product, invited 5 teammates, and hit a usage threshold. 

The sales conversation changes from 'Let me pitch you our software' to 'I noticed your team created 20 boards this week and is hitting the workspace storage limit; let me help you unlock enterprise SSO and compliance.' Closing rates on PQLs are 3x to 5x higher than traditional MQLs."
