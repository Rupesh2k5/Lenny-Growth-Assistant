### On the 4-Step PMF Engine
**Rahul Vohra**:
"When we started Superhuman, our Sean Ellis PMF score was only 22%. If we had launched publicly then, we would have failed. We spent two years systematically building a machine to optimize that score from 22% to 58%.

Here is the 4-step PMF Engine:

1. **Segment to Find Your High-Expectation Customers (HXC)**:
   - Filter your PMF survey responses. Look only at users who answered 'Very Disappointed'. Look at their job titles, usage frequency, and workflows.
   - Example: For Superhuman, we realized founders, executives, investors, and sales leads loved us; casual newsletter readers did not. We redefined our target audience around this HXC persona.

2. **Analyze Feedback to Convert 'Somewhat Disappointed' Users**:
   - Don't listen to people who answered 'Not Disappointed'—they want features that distract from your core value.
   - Look at the users who answered 'Somewhat Disappointed' AND whose main benefit aligned with your HXC group. Ask them: *'What holds you back from loving this product?'* Usually, it's 1 or 2 missing features (e.g. mobile app, calendar integration).

3. **Divide Your Roadmap 50/50**:
   - Spend **50%** of your engineering resources doubling down on what your 'Very Disappointed' users already love (speed, keyboard shortcuts, offline mode).
   - Spend **50%** of your resources addressing the specific blockers named by the 'Somewhat Disappointed' group.

4. **Re-survey Regularly and Track the Trend**:
   - Measure your PMF score across each cohort every quarter. When the score crosses 40%, you have green light to scale paid marketing and referral loops."
